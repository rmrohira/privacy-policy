Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how our internal productivity applications and automations handle user data when integrating with Google APIs (including Google Sheets, Google Drive, and Gmail).

1. Data Accessed and Used

Our applications access Google user data strictly to perform authorized business automation tasks on behalf of the authenticated user:

  • Google Sheets & Drive: Reading inventory files, purchase orders, sales reports, and appending calculated material requirements.
  • Gmail: Reading supplier status emails and sending automated internal notifications directly as instructed by the user.

2. Data Storage and Security

  • We do not store, sell, trade, or transfer your Google user data or credentials to third parties or advertising brokers.
  • All OAuth tokens and API credentials are kept locally on secured internal machines and workstations under strict access controls.
  • Data processed during workflow runs is retained only as long as necessary to complete the automated calculation or reporting task.

3. Google API User Data Policy Compliance

Our applications' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
